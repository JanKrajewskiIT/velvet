Fixed-ratio frame for an image, video or embed. Reserves space before load (no layout shift), shimmer while loading, striped placeholder when empty or failed, glass play button over a video (with or without poster), top-right badge (duration, "Live"), bottom overlay on a scrim, caption + credit. Ratios come from --aspect-*, styling from --media-*.

**Props.** The full contract is `MediaFrameProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.
