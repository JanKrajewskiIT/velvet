Light/dark switch — two overlapping rings with filled sun/moon; the selected ring fills and scales up with a light spin on click. Uncontrolled by default: sets html[data-theme] and persists via the Velvet theme key (localStorage 'vui-theme'), staying in sync with other toggles. Pass value+onChange to control. variant: light (default, on light/neutral surfaces) · navbar (on Navbar tone="brand" / brand fills) · dark (on dark or media surfaces).

**Props.** The full contract is `ThemeToggleProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.

**Built from:** Glyph. **CSS:** `.vui-tt*`. **Preview:** `components/ThemeToggle/preview.html` (group Actions).
