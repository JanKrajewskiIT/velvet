Vertical list whose items are joined by a connector line under their leading marker (Marker). Timeline renders it; use for activity feeds, audit logs, step lists.

**Props.** The full contract is `RailProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.

- Also accepts the native attributes of the root element.

**Also exports:** RailItem. **CSS:** `.vui-rail*`. **Preview:** `components/Rail/preview.html` (group Atoms).
