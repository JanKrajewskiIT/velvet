Video with Velvet controls over a bottom scrim: play, seek (buffered range shown), mute + volume, time, captions, speed, full screen. Controls fade after 2.5 s of stillness while playing. Keyboard on the focused player: Space / K play · ← → 5 s · J / L 10 s · M mute · F full screen · C captions. Same ratio scale and play button as MediaFrame — use MediaFrame kind="video" for a static thumbnail that opens a player elsewhere.

**Props.** The full contract is `VideoPlayerProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.

**Built from:** Glyph, `media/time.js`. **Also exports:** PlayerButton. **CSS:** `.vui-player*`. **Preview:** `components/VideoPlayer/preview.html` (group Media).
