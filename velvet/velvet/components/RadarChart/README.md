Compares 1–3 profiles across 5–8 dimensions — skill profiles, product scores, team health. Legend toggles series, hovering a vertex dims the others and shows the value. Over 3 series or 8 axes, use a BarChart instead.

**Props.** The full contract is `RadarChartProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.

**CSS:** `.vui-radar*`. **Preview:** `components/RadarChart/preview.html` (group Charts).

**Built from:** ChartLegend, ChartTooltip.
