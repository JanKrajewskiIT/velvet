Selection tray for "pick 2–4 things, then compare" (contacts, proposals, plans). Slides up from the bottom when something is selected (floating) or sits inline. Shows removable chips, empty slots up to min, a live hint ("Select 1 more…", "Compare up to 4…") and Compare — disabled outside min…max.

**Props.** The full contract is `ComparisonBarProps` in `components/index.d.ts`.

**Built from:** Button, Glyph, CloseButton, Dialog, RadarChart, Avatar. **Also exports:** ComparisonDialog. **CSS:** `.vui-cmpbar*`. **Preview:** `components/ComparisonBar/preview.html` (group Metrics).
